# Public money trail

Every campaign page shows, from the [[Ledger]]: total raised, fees taken,
each withdrawal (date, amount, bank name, last 4 digits) and the balance left.
No competitor does this, and it's the strongest answer to "how do I know the
money went where they said?" See [[Competitive research 2026-11]].
