# AI story writer

Creator answers a few questions (who, what happened, how much, what the money
is for) and the `AiProvider` drafts a title, short description and story,
streamed into the editor for the creator to review. The creator always
publishes; AI never publishes on its own. Optional: disabled when no AI key is
set.
