# Reports and moderation

Public job: flag a concerning campaign without signing in. A quiet disclosure
below the story opens a labelled category/details form, with a clear private
submission promise. Nothing is automatically removed because a report exists.

Admin job: inspect allegations, review campaign context, take a bounded action
and record why. Extend the existing ivory/forest review workspace with a Reports
tab, compact rows, amber open-state indicators and explicit action labels. Keep
allegations visually distinct from established campaign facts. No new packages.

Targets: 1440x1000 and 390x844. Stack report detail and action controls on mobile.
States: disabled submitting controls, private receipt, empty queue, recoverable
error/stale state, failed media with story retained, and permission denial.
