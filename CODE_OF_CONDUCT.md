# Code of conduct

OpenRaise welcomes contributors of every background and experience level.
This code applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and other project
spaces, and when representing the project publicly.

Treat people with respect. Offer specific, constructive feedback, assume
questions are asked in good faith, and respect different experiences and
access needs. Critique work and ideas without attacking the person.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual attention or imagery, personal
attacks, and publishing someone's private information without permission are
unacceptable. Repeated disruption and retaliation against someone who reports
a concern are also unacceptable.

If you experience or witness a violation, contact a repository maintainer
privately through a contact method on their public profile. If the concern
involves that maintainer, contact another maintainer or the repository owner.
Share only the information needed to understand what happened. Do not place
sensitive reports in public issues.

Maintainers will review reports, seek context where appropriate, and handle
personal information discreetly. Depending on severity and repetition, they
may request a correction, remove content, issue a warning, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation. Serious threats or harassment may result
in an immediate ban. Anyone subject to an action may request reconsideration
privately from a maintainer who was not involved, when one is available.
